What counts — and what doesn't

✅ Included (savings & investments):
  • Bank deposits (current & savings accounts)
  • Mutual funds, bonds, publicly-traded shares
  • Managed investment accounts
  • Private pension plans & whole-life insurance (cash value)
  • Money lent to others (private loans)
  • Precious metals, options/futures, other financial assets
❌ Not included (these are “real assets”):
  • Your house / main residence
  • Other real estate property
  • Cars, vehicles, boats
  • Valuables (jewelry, art, etc.)
  • A business you actively run (self-employment)
How this is calculated
  • Your percentile is read from the official 2023 household financial-assets distribution (ECB Household Finance & Consumption Survey, run for Greece by the Bank of Greece), interpolated between the published deciles (P10–P90).
  • Median = €3,000; 90th percentile = €31,900. (The mean, €14,200, is far above the median — wealth is heavily skewed.)
  • Figures are per household (not adjusted for size) and are conditional on households that hold at least some financial asset (≈99% of households).
  • Below P10 (€200) is shown as “bottom 10%”; above P90 (€31,900) as “top 10%” — the exact percentiles beyond those points aren't published.
Source: ECB HFCS 2023 statistical tables, Table C3 · See docs/SOURCES.md.
