How this is calculated
  • Equivalised income = household net income ÷ (1 + 0.5 × extra adults + 0.3 × children). This adjusts for household size (modified-OECD scale).
  • Your percentile is read from the official 2025 equivalised-income distribution (Eurostat EU-SILC dataset ilc_di01), interpolated between the published quantile points (1st–99th percentile).
  • Median (50th percentile) = €11,700 / year; 90th percentile = €20,796 / year.
  • Values below the 1st or above the 99th percentile are shown as “bottom/top 1%” (the exact quantiles are not published beyond those points).
  • This measures household living standard, not individual gross salary.
Sources: Eurostat ilc_di01 and ilc_di03 · ELSTAT “Income and Living Conditions” (SFA10). See docs/SOURCES.md in this repository.
